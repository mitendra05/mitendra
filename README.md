## 🏗️ What I've built

Most of my professional work lives in private company repositories. Here is what's behind them.

### Axipays: payment gateway, built from scratch
I owned the build from March 2023, and it went to production **in three months**.

- **Card processing:** led delivery of 3DS and non-3DS flows across server-to-server, hosted page and iFrame integrations
- **Acquirer integrations:** Mastercard Payment Gateway Services (MPGS), Visa Cybersource and Redsys (Spain), with a new acquirer shipping in **under 24 hours**
- **Scalability and reliability:** led delivery of a Go backend behind a load balancer, with Redis caching, duplicate-charge prevention, and a polling service that resolves pending transactions so gateway and bank state stay consistent
- **Risk and fraud:** fraud scoring, device fingerprinting and risk rules that cut data-quality errors by **90%**, plus **merchant URL tracing**, browser-side JavaScript I designed and built myself to detect transaction laundering
- **Dynamic Currency Conversion:** took card acceptance from 2 currencies to **57+**
- **Money movement back office:** automated refunds and settlements, and **reconciliation logic** I wrote myself that removed **16+ hours** of manual matching every week
- **Compliance:** ran the product and architecture walkthroughs for PCI DSS Level 1 validation, completed in three months
- **Infrastructure:** AWS, Cloudflare, and CI/CD with GitHub Actions
- **Portals:** website, admin, merchant and referral portals
